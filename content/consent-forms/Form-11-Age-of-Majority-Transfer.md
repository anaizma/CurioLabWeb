> **Read this first.** You are turning eighteen, so decisions that your parent or guardian made for you now become yours. This form moves control of your account and your choices to you. Nothing here is required to keep taking part, but until you make these choices yourself, we pause the optional permissions your guardian had set, so that you are the one deciding.

---

## 1. What changes

- Control of your CurioLab account transfers to you.
- Your guardian's authority to consent on your behalf ends for anything collected or published from this point on.
- Permissions your guardian previously set for publication, direct messaging, and likeness are paused until you renew them in your own name.

- **Type:** adult self-consent; supersedes guardian authority prospectively.
