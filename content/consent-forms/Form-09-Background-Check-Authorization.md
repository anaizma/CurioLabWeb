## Section 3: Scope and your rights

- The report may include criminal history, sex-offender registry results, and identity verification, as permitted by law and appropriate to a role involving access to minors.
- If CurioLab considers taking adverse action based in whole or in part on a report, CurioLab will first provide you a copy of the report and a summary of your rights, and give you an opportunity to respond, before making a final decision.
- If CurioLab takes final adverse action, it will notify you and identify the reporting agency.
- You may dispute the accuracy of a report with the reporting agency.

## Section 4: Ohio youth-serving overlay (for counsel)

If CurioLab is classified as an institution or association under Ohio Revised Code Chapter 5103, additional screening (including a national sex-offender-public-website search and a child-welfare summary report) may be required before engaging anyone with access to children. Classification is reserved for counsel.
