## 1. Role and status

1.1. The volunteer serves in the role stated below, under the supervision described, without expectation of employment, wages, or benefits unless separately agreed in writing.

**Role:** _________________  **Chapter:** _________________  **Reports to:** _________________

1.2. The volunteer's authority is limited to their role and does not extend to binding CurioLab beyond it.

## 2. Conditions of service

2.1. Service is conditioned on completing and keeping current all required screening (Form 9) and training, and, for Mentors, on the Code of Conduct (Form 8).

2.2. The volunteer will follow CurioLab policies, including the professional-boundary, communication, safety, and reporting rules, and will not act in a way that contradicts Form 8.

## 3. Confidentiality, data, and intellectual property

3.1. The volunteer will keep Student and program information confidential and use it only for their role.

3.2. Ownership of program materials and project work is as CurioLab specifies in writing; personal or Student-owned work is not claimed by this section.

3.3. On the end of the role, the volunteer will return or destroy program data as directed, except records CurioLab must preserve.

## 4. Term and termination

4.1. The role is at-will and may be ended by either party at any time, prospectively.

4.2. Termination or a data-deletion request does not require CurioLab to destroy incident, screening, or message records it must preserve.

4.3. This Agreement is reviewed at least annually as an operational practice.
