# Form 6: Emergency Contact and Medical Authorization (In-Person Activities)

**DRAFT FOR ATTORNEY REVIEW. NOT A FINAL FORM. NOT LEGAL ADVICE.**
Document ID: CL-CONSENT-06 · Version 2026.03 (draft) · Governing law: Ohio and applicable federal law
Applies only when your child attends in-person activities. Incorporates Form 00 by reference.
**Counsel note:** the scope and validity of any delegated emergency-treatment authority for CurioLab's specific venues and staffing is an open legal question. This draft is intentionally narrow and is not a liability waiver.

> **Read this first.** If your child attends an in-person session, we need to know how to reach you and how to respond in an emergency. This form lets us call for emergency help and share the information you provide with the people responding. It is not permission for CurioLab to make ongoing medical decisions for your child, and it does not replace a doctor's own consent process.

### Annex (for CurioLab / counsel)

- **Grant:** `emergency_medical` (required for in-person participation; not a COPPA grant).
- **Verification:** verified guardian signature; strong identity confirmation prudent though not COPPA-driven.
- **Renewal:** annually and on any health-information change.
- **Retention:** limited to safety, incident-response, and any claims period; restrict access to health data.
- **Prohibited:** any release of liability for negligence; any overbroad delegation of medical authority.
- **Open question for counsel:** enforceable scope of emergency-treatment authorization for CurioLab's venues, staff, and activities. No general Ohio statute located that makes a nonprofit's generic form dispositive.
