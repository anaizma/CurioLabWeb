# Form 8: Mentor Code of Conduct and Acceptable Use Agreement

**DRAFT FOR ATTORNEY REVIEW. NOT A FINAL FORM. NOT LEGAL ADVICE.**
Document ID: CL-CONSENT-08 · Version 2026.03 (draft) · Governing law: Ohio and applicable federal law
**Adult agreement. This is not a guardian consent form and is not governed by COPPA.** Incorporates Form 00 by reference.

This Agreement is between CurioLab and the undersigned Mentor. By signing, the Mentor agrees to the following as a condition of program access.

---

## 1. Role and boundaries

1.1. The Mentor works with Students only within their assigned role and chapter, and only through CurioLab's Platform and program channels.

1.2. The Mentor will maintain professional boundaries at all times and will not engage in any romantic, sexual, or otherwise inappropriate conduct with a Student, nor any conduct that grooms, isolates, or unduly influences a Student.

1.3. The Mentor will not communicate privately with a Student outside CurioLab's supervised channels, will not exchange personal contact information, and will not move any conversation to a personal phone, email, social account, or outside application.

1.4. The Mentor will not arrange in-person contact with a Student outside sanctioned program activities, and will not offer gifts, rides, money, or favors.

## 2. Communication rules

2.1. All Mentor-to-Student messaging occurs on the Platform, is retained, and is subject to review. The Mentor will not attempt to delete, conceal, or circumvent message retention.

2.2. The Mentor will not ask a Student to keep any communication secret from the Student's Guardian, the Safety Officer, or program staff.

2.3. The Mentor will observe posted messaging hours and content limits.

## 3. Safety and reporting

3.1. The Mentor will promptly escalate any safety concern to the Director, regardless of whether the Mentor is certain of any legal duty.

3.2. If the Mentor is a mandatory reporter under Ohio Revised Code 2151.421 in their actual role or profession, the Mentor will report as that statute requires, and understands that no internal process may delay a required report. (Whether a given Mentor is a statutory mandatory reporter depends on that Mentor's actual role and is determined with counsel; this Agreement does not represent that every Mentor holds that status.)

3.3. The Mentor will preserve, and not alter, any material relevant to a safety concern.

## 4. Confidentiality and data

4.1. The Mentor will access Student information only as needed for their role, will keep it confidential, and will not disclose or use it outside the program.

4.2. On the end of the Mentor's role, the Mentor will cease access and return or destroy program data as directed, except records CurioLab must preserve.

## 5. Screening and training

5.1. The Mentor's access is conditioned on completing required background screening and training before working with Students, and on keeping them current.

5.2. Access ends automatically if screening or training lapses.

## 6. Term and consequences

6.1. This Agreement takes effect before program access and is renewed at least annually and after any material policy change.

6.2. CurioLab may suspend or end the Mentor's access for any violation, and violations may carry legal consequences.
