# Form 1: Enrollment and Account Setup

**DRAFT FOR ATTORNEY REVIEW. NOT A FINAL FORM. NOT LEGAL ADVICE.**
Document ID: CL-CONSENT-01 · Version 2026.03 (draft) · Governing law: Ohio and applicable federal law
Incorporates Form 00 (Master Terms) by reference. Capitalized terms are defined there.

> **How this form works.** This form has three parts. Each part has its own boxes and its own signature. Check the boxes you agree to. Leave blank anything you do not agree to, and we will record that as a "no." Your signature at the end of each part confirms only the boxes you checked in that part. Parts A and B are required to enroll. Part C is required to use the online Platform, but the pieces inside it are yours to choose.

## Part C: Platform account and information we collect (account consent required; the second item is optional)

**Plain summary.** To use the Platform, your child needs an account. Below is what we collect, why, and who sees it. By default nothing your child creates is public. Public sharing, photographs, and Mentor messaging are handled on their own separate forms, not here.

**Information collected and its purpose:**

| Information | Purpose | Who can see it |
|---|---|---|
| Name or a nickname you choose | To identify the account | Student, Mentor, program staff |
| Age or grade level | To match curriculum and apply age-based protections | Program staff |
| Project work the Student creates | Core purpose of the program | Student, Mentor; the chapter only if you allow the optional item below |
| Attendance and Mentor verifications | To build the Student's record | Student, Mentor, program staff |
| Login and basic technical data | Account operation and security | Program staff and service providers who help operate the Platform |

**What this form does not do.** It does not make your child's work public, does not release it to outside parties, and does not enable Mentor direct messaging. Each of those has its own form.

Please choose:

- [ ] **(Required to use the Platform.)** I consent to CurioLab creating an account for my child and collecting and using the information above, for the stated purposes and for Internal use only.
- [ ] **(Optional.)** My child's project work may also be visible to other Students and Mentors within my child's own chapter when they are signed in. This remains non-public and is not searchable on the internet. Leave blank to keep the work visible only to the Mentor and staff.

**Your ongoing rights.** You may review the information we hold about your child, request its deletion, or withdraw the optional item above at any time. Withdrawing the required account consent closes the account. Certain records we are required to keep are described in the child privacy notice and in Form 00.

> The first item is required to use the Platform. If you leave it blank, your child may still take part in in-person activities without an online account. The second item is optional.
