# Form 00: Master Terms, Definitions, and Execution Standards

**DRAFT FOR ATTORNEY REVIEW. NOT A FINAL FORM. NOT LEGAL ADVICE.**
Document ID: CL-CONSENT-00 · Version 2026.03 (draft) · Program jurisdiction: Ohio
Governing law: laws of the State of Ohio and applicable federal law.

> **Purpose of this document.** This is the shared front matter for the CurioLab consent and agreement package (Forms 1 through 11). Each individual form incorporates the definitions and standards below by reference so that the forms themselves stay short and readable. This document is not signed by guardians. It is a controlling reference for CurioLab staff and reviewing counsel.

---

## 1. Defined terms

The following terms have the same meaning in every form in this package.

- **CurioLab, we, us, our.** The Ohio nonprofit organization operating the CurioLab mentorship program and platform. (Entity name and status to be finalized on IRS determination.)
- **Platform.** The CurioLab web application, including student and guardian portals, project tools, and any messaging or posting features.
- **Student, child.** A person enrolled in the program. Where a form distinguishes by age, it does so expressly.
- **Guardian.** A parent or other person with legal authority to consent on behalf of a Student who is a minor.
- **Mentor.** A screened adult who works directly with Students.
- **Director.** A person with administrative authority over a chapter.
- **Safety Officer.** A designated adult, who is not a Mentor or Student, responsible for reviewing safety matters, including messaging where enabled.
- **Grant.** A specific, separately recorded permission created when a Guardian or eligible signer affirmatively checks a consent item. Each Grant is recorded independently and, unless a form states otherwise, is independently revocable.
- **Internal use.** Collection or use of a Student's information solely to operate the program, visible only to the Student, their Mentor, and program staff, and not disclosed publicly or to outside parties.
- **Public disclosure.** Making a Student's information available to persons outside CurioLab's internal operation, including on a public web page or newsletter.
- **Elevated form.** A form authorizing Public disclosure or adult-to-minor direct contact. Elevated forms require a stronger identity-verification method and are never a condition of participation.

## 2. Verification standard (to be finalized by counsel)

CurioLab records consent electronically. The verification method scales to the sensitivity of the Grant:

- **Standard grants** (Internal use only): guardian identity confirmed by the method counsel approves for that tier.
- **Elevated grants** (Public disclosure or direct messaging): a stronger method reasonably designed to confirm the signer is the Guardian, such as a signed form returned to CurioLab, a payment-card confirmation, or a live video confirmation. A checkbox alone does not satisfy an Elevated grant.

**Standing note for counsel.** Because the Platform allows account creation, social interaction, and content posting by users under sixteen, both the federal Children's Online Privacy Protection Rule (for users under thirteen) and Ohio Revised Code 1349.09 (for users under sixteen) are potentially in scope, and their verification duties are not limited to the same ages. CurioLab's status as a covered operator is a threshold question reserved for counsel. Pending that determination, the package assumes strong verification for users under sixteen on Elevated grants.

## 3. Electronic signature and evidence

Each executed form is captured with an audit record that includes, at minimum: the final document version and its content hash; every consent item presented and its checked or unchecked state; the timestamp of each action; the signer's name, claimed relationship, and contact; the identity-verification method used and its result; the signer's agreement to transact electronically; delivery and view events; signature time and session metadata; confirmation that a copy was made available to the signer; and any later revocation. This record is designed to support enforceability under the federal E-SIGN Act and Ohio Revised Code Chapter 1306. Electronic execution applies only where the signer has agreed to transact electronically and can retain a copy.

## 4. Revocation

Unless a specific form states that a Grant is required for participation or must be preserved by law, a Guardian may revoke any Grant at any time through the guardian portal or by written request. Revocation operates prospectively. It stops future collection, use, or disclosure under that Grant and, where applicable, triggers removal from surfaces CurioLab controls within a reasonable time. Revocation does not compel deletion of records CurioLab must retain for legal, safety, or audit reasons, and does not retrieve copies already made by third parties.

## 5. Retention and preservation

CurioLab retains records only as long as needed for the purpose collected, plus any period required by law, insurance, claim, investigation, or litigation hold. One category is treated specially: **direct-messaging records** and their related reports and access logs are retained until the Student reaches age thirty, consistent with the Ohio limitations period for claims arising from childhood sexual abuse (Ohio Revised Code 2305.111). This retention overrides an ordinary deletion request and is disclosed in the relevant form.

## 6. Reporting and safety

CurioLab adults are instructed to escalate reasonable safety concerns to the Director without delay. Where an individual is a mandatory reporter under Ohio Revised Code 2151.421 in their actual role, that person must report as the statute requires, and no internal process may delay a required report. CurioLab cannot promise confidentiality where a report is legally required.

## 7. Order of precedence

If a specific form conflicts with this master document, the specific form controls for that form's subject, except that the safety, reporting, and preservation provisions in sections 5 and 6 always control.
